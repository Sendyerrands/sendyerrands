Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026 · Last updated 8 September 2026

Sendy Errands (“Sendy”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using the Sendy Errands mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when we share it, how long we retain it, and the rights available to you.

By using Sendy Errands, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Customers who request errands, deliveries, purchases, or related services;
  • Riders or delivery personnel who accept and perform errands;
  • Merchants, stores, vendors, and other businesses involved in fulfilling errands;
  • Visitors to the Sendy website;
  • Users who contact Sendy through support channels;
  • Individuals whose information is legitimately provided to us in connection with a transaction.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Sendy, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Full name;
  • Phone number;
  • Email address;
  • Profile photograph, where applicable;
  • Password or authentication information;
  • Account type or role;
  • Account creation and verification information.

We only request information reasonably necessary to create and operate your account.

2.2 Delivery and transaction information

When you create or accept an errand, we may collect:

  • Pickup location;
  • Delivery location;
  • Delivery instructions;
  • Description of the requested item or service;
  • Customer’s estimated item price;
  • Rider’s delivery-price offer;
  • Accepted delivery price;
  • Merchant/store information;
  • Order or errand status;
  • Transaction reference;
  • Receipts, where provided;
  • Delivery confirmation;
  • Dispute information;
  • Cancellation information.

Users should not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in delivery instructions.

2.3 Location information

Sendy may collect location information where necessary to provide location-based services. This may include:

  • Approximate location;
  • Precise GPS location, where you grant permission;
  • Pickup and delivery coordinates;
  • Rider location during an active delivery;
  • Location associated with a completed transaction.

Location information may be used to:

  • Match riders with errands;
  • Display relevant delivery information;
  • Estimate routes and distances;
  • Provide delivery tracking;
  • Detect abnormal or fraudulent activity;
  • Improve service reliability;
  • Resolve disputes concerning pickup or delivery.

You may disable device location permissions through your device settings. Certain functionality may not work correctly when location permissions are disabled.

3. Payment information

Sendy may facilitate payments for errands through third-party payment providers. Depending on the payment method, we may process information such as:

  • Payment status;
  • Transaction reference;
  • Amount paid;
  • Currency;
  • Payment method;
  • Refund status;
  • Payment timestamps;
  • Merchant settlement information.

Where payment processing is performed by a third-party payment service provider, card numbers, CVV numbers, PINs, and other sensitive payment credentials may be handled directly by that provider rather than stored by Sendy.

Sendy does not intentionally store full payment-card credentials unless specifically required and lawfully implemented for a particular service.

Sendy’s payment provider may have its own privacy policy and terms governing payment processing.

  • Browser type;
  • IP address;
  • Device identifiers;
  • Network information;
  • Crash logs;
  • Error information;
  • Approximate geographic information;

This information helps us secure, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the Platform.

Sendy is a two-sided marketplace/service platform. Some information must be shared between users to perform an errand.

For example, after an errand is accepted, a rider may need access to:

  • Customer name;
  • Pickup information;
  • Delivery information;
  • Relevant delivery instructions;
  • Phone/contact functionality;
  • Information about the requested item.

Similarly, a customer may receive information about the assigned rider necessary to identify or communicate with the rider.

Sendy attempts to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary to perform the transaction.

17. Deleting your account

You may request deletion of your Sendy Errands account at any time:

  • In the app — open Profile, then Delete account, and follow the confirmation steps.
  • By email — write to support@sendyerrands.com from the address registered to your account.

When an account is deleted, we remove or anonymise your profile information, saved addresses, and contact details. As described in section 15, certain transaction, payment and security records are retained where we are required to keep them for accounting, legal, regulatory, or fraud-prevention purposes; those records stop being linked to your profile.

Accounts with an outstanding order, an unresolved dispute, or an outstanding balance may need those matters concluded before deletion completes.
